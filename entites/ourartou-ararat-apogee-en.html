Ourartou / Ararat (apogée)

Year on the map : -700

Under Argishti I and Sarduri II (8th c. BC), the kingdom stretched from Sevan to Urmia and from the Euphrates to the Zab — Assyria's main rival. Sargon II dealt it a severe blow in 714 (sack of Musasir). It vanished around 590, absorbed by the Medes. "Ararat" is the biblical form of the same name (Genesis 8:4); a state of Urartian language, it is the territorial and demographic bedrock of ancient Armenia: the Behistun inscription (~520) matches "Armina" with "Urashtu", and in the dominant hypothesis (Diakonoff) proto-Armenians absorbed its population after its fall.
