Épire (royaume molosse)

Year on the map : -323

Independent kingdom of the Molossian kings (Aeacid dynasty) — Molossis around Dodona, Chaonia and Thesprotia on the Ionian coast. Linked to Macedon by dynastic ties (Olympias, Alexander's mother, was a Molossian princess), it remained outside its administration: its own army and coinage, absent from the empire's provincial lists (Diodorus Siculus XVIII.3). Alexander I of Epirus died in Italy in 331 BC; his nephew Aeacides succeeded him and would support Olympias against Cassander in 317 BC. Northern and eastern borders (toward Illyria and Upper Macedonia) are approximate, not fixed at this date.
