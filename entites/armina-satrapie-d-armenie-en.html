Armina (satrapie d'Arménie)

Year on the map : -400

First mention of the name: "Armina" in Darius I's Behistun inscription (~520 BC). An Achaemenid satrapy of the highland, it paid silver tribute (Herodotus III.93) and supplied prized horses (20,000 foals a year according to Strabo); Xenophon crossed it in 401-400 (Anabasis IV), describing its semi-underground villages and barley wine.
