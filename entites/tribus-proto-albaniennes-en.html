Tribus proto-albaniennes

Year on the map : -705

On the left bank of the lower Kura and the Alazani (the future Shirvan/Aghvank), Caucasian Iron Age tribes occupied in the 8th-7th centuries BC the plains of the northern bank. They form the substrate of the twenty-six peoples that ancient authors would attach to Caucasian Albania — but the Albanian political entity would only take shape in the Hellenistic era.
