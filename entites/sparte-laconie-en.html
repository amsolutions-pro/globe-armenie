Sparte (Laconie)

Year on the map : -323

Independent city-state of Laconia: it refused to join the League of Corinth from its founding (337 BC) and never came under Macedonian rule or Alexander's empire. In 331 BC, King Agis III led a Pan-Hellenic revolt, crushed by the regent Antipater at Megalopolis (Diodorus Siculus XVII.62-63; Curtius Rufus VI.1): Sparta had to hand over hostages but kept its own kings (Eudamidas I) and laws, with no Macedonian garrison. Neighbouring Messenia, independent since 369 BC, is excluded from the outline.
