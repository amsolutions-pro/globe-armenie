RSS d'Arménie (république de plein droit)

Year on the map : 1936

The 1936 Soviet constitution dissolved the Transcaucasian Federation: Armenia became a full union republic of the USSR. The same year the Great Terror began — the Armenian intelligentsia (Charents, Bakunts) was decimated; Aghasi Khanjian died in July 1936.
