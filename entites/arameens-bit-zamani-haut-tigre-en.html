Araméens (Bit-Zamani, haut-Tigre)

Year on the map : -1000

An Aramean principality of the upper Tigris (Amida/Diyarbakır, Tushhan), attested in the 11th-9th centuries BC on the south-western margin of the plateau, just south of the Mushki. It illustrates the Semitic (Aramean, green) contribution penetrating the plateau from the south — the counterpart of the Indo-European Mushki stream. Conquered by Ashurnasirpal II around 879 BC, it was absorbed into Assyria.
