Khazars

Year on the map : 885

In this dataset's 900 entry specifically, the Khazars' southern border sits roughly two degrees of latitude further north than in the neighbouring 885 and 1000 entries of this same dataset, leaving a gap between its outline and the Caspian/Black Sea — uncovered by any territory or by the seas themselves, this gap renders as plain ocean blue and can create the misleading illusion of a link between the two seas (visitor report, 2026-08-04). This is a source-basemap simplification/discrepancy specific to this one year: the Khazar khaganate genuinely held, both in reality and in the neighbouring entries of this dataset, the North Caucasus land corridor separating the two seas — this notice applies only to the 900 occurrence.
