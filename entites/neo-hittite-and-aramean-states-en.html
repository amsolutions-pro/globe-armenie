Neo-Hittite and Aramean states

Year on the map : -800

A mosaic of kingdoms (Tabal, Melid, Kummuh, Carchemish, Hamath...) heirs to the Hittite Empire after its fall around 1180 BC, blending Luwian (hieroglyphic) and Aramean traditions. Assyria annexed nearly all of them between 740 and 708 BC.
