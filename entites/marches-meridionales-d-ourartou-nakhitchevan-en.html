Marches méridionales d'Ourartou (Nakhitchevan)

Year on the map : -705

The plain of Nakhichevan and the southern bank of the middle Araxes formed, around 800 BC, a southern march that Menua of Urartu began to subdue, as attested by his inscriptions in the Araxes valley. These lands, peopled by local highland communities with no kingdom of their own at this date, would be fully integrated into Urartu under Argishti I.
