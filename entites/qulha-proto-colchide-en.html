Qulha (proto-Colchide)

Year on the map : -705

Proto-Colchian ancestor of classical Colchis, centred on the lower Chorokh and Adjara, known from the annals of Sarduri II, who took its royal city Ildamusha around 750 BC. Ravaged by the Cimmerians at the end of the 8th century, its legacy later re-formed in the Colchis of the Rioni.
