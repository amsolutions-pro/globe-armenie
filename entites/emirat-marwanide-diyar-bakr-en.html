Émirat marwanide (Diyar Bakr)

Year on the map : 1064

Kurdish dynasty (983-1085) ruling Diyar Bakr from Mayyafariqin (Silvan) and Amida (Diyarbakır). Southern neighbour of the Armenian kingdoms, it held Akhlat on the north-west shore of Lake Van. A Seljuk vassal after 1055, it fell in 1085 when Malik Shah entrusted the region to Fakhr al-Dawla ibn Jahir.
