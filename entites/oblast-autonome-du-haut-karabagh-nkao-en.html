Oblast autonome du Haut-Karabagh (NKAO)

Year on the map : 1923

On 7 July 1923, Nagorno-Karabakh, ~94% Armenian, was constituted as an autonomous oblast within Soviet Azerbaijan — after the party's Caucasian bureau decision (5 July 1921) to attach it to Baku. This contested status would feed the conflict at the century's end.
