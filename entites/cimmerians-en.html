Cimmerians

Year on the map : -700

A horse people from the Pontic steppe (the Gimirri of Assyrian sources), the Cimmerians crossed the Caucasus in the 8th century BC and became the great nomadic threat to the Armenian highland and eastern Anatolia: they clashed with Rusa I of Urartu around 714 BC, then killed the Assyrian king Sargon II in battle in 705 BC, before ravaging Lydia and dispersing into Cappadocia in the 7th century (Herodotus IV, Assyrian annals) — a nomadic people without a state or fixed borders; the shown area is an approximate raiding zone, not an organized territory. At -705, an invasion south of the Caucasus (Urartu/Lake Van–Tabal/Cappadocia corridor) extends the original steppe into one continuous territory, illustrating this crossing at the height of the crisis; at -800 and -700, only the original Pontic-Caspian steppe is shown, since this one-off raid left no lastingly held presence in the south.
