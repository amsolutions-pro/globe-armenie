Mushki (apport indo-européen)

Year on the map : -800

Thraco-Phrygian tribes (Mushki/Mushku, the Moschoi of the Greeks) who came from the Balkans after the Bronze Age collapse (~1200 BC). Their eastern branch, attested from ~1165 BC by Tiglath-Pileser I ("20,000 Mushki and their five kings" holding Alzi and Purukuzzi for fifty years), settled on the south-western margins of the plateau (upper Tigris, Isuwa, Sophene); the western branch formed the Phrygia of Mita/Midas. It carries the Indo-European contribution (family akin to the Hittites and Greeks, in blue-violet) which, merged into the autochthonous Hurro-Urartian substrate, feeds the Armenian ethnogenesis — a contribution, not a sole origin (Diakonoff's debated thesis).
