Bassin de Van — tribus hourrites (futur Nairi)

Year on the map : -1500

The name "Nairi" only appears in Assyrian sources with Tukulti-Ninurta I (~1240 BC, "43 kings of the lands of Nairi"). At 1500 BC the map shows, as an acknowledged retrojection, the Hurrian tribes of the Van basin that would form these "lands of Nairi", then the kingdom of Urartu. No border is attested at this date.
