Culture de Lchashen-Metsamor

Year on the map : -1000

A Late Bronze/Early Iron Age horizon (~1500-800 BC), directly succeeding the Trialeti-Vanadzor culture over the same area: the chariot burials of Lchashen by Lake Sevan, the fortress-sanctuary of Metsamor in the Ararat plain, characteristic incised black and grey pottery, and the rise of cyclopean fortresses (Smith, Badalyan & Avetisyan; Kushnareva; Sagona). Continuity of settlement with the Middle Bronze Age substrate; Kartli and Colchis develop their own regional facies in parallel, already covered separately on this map. A non-state material culture spanning the central highland, the Sevan basin, Nakhichevan and northern Karabakh — no border is attested — which declines with the Urartian campaigns of Argishti I in the late 8th century BC.
