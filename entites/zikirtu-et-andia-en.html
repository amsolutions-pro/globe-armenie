Zikirtu et Andia

Year on the map : -705

East and northeast of Lake Urmia, between the Zagros foothills and the Caspian: the annals of Sargon II of Assyria ("Letter to the God Ashur," eighth campaign, 714 BC) place here the kingdoms of Zikirtu (King Mitatti) and Andia, allies in turn of Urartu against Assyria. Their earliest attestation dates to this campaign; their presence as early as the 8th century BC is a plausible continuity but not directly documented — approximate area, fluctuating tributary borders.
