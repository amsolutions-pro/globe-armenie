Arménie occidentale (massacres d'Adana)

Year on the map : 1909

In April 1909, amid the Ottoman counter-revolution, the city of Adana and the Cilician plain went up in flames: 20,000 to 30,000 Armenians were killed within weeks. Neither the inquiries nor the trials that followed produced real justice (Kévorkian, Der Matossian).
