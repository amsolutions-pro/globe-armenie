Culture de Trialeti-Vanadzor

Year on the map : -1500

In the Middle Bronze Age (~2200-1500 BC), the northern highland and the South Caucasus were peopled by the Trialeti-Vanadzor culture, famed for its princely kurgans — Trialeti (gold cup, chariots), Vanadzor/Lori Berd, Lchashen by Lake Sevan — evidence of strong social stratification and tin-bronze metallurgy. It succeeded the Kura-Araxes culture with continuity of settlement, and comprised regional facies (Karmir Berd, Sevan-Uzerlik, Kizil-Vank). This Bronze Age substrate is often proposed as one of the crucibles of the highland's Armenian ethnogenesis — a hypothesis, not a demonstrated fact.
