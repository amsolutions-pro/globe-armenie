Cultures Trialeti et du Sevan

Year on the map : -2000

Middle Bronze Age (~2000-1500 BC): villages gave way to spectacular princely kurgans (Trialeti, Karashamb, Lchashen) with gold and silver cups, chariots and ceremonial weapons — the rise of warrior elites on the highland and around Lake Sevan. Neither Hayasa-Azzi (attested from -1500) nor the Nairi confederations (from -1000) exist yet under that name at this date — this archaeological culture is their substrate, without a directly attested political continuity.
