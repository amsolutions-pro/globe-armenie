Principauté zakaride sous l'Ilkhanat

Year on the map : 1279

The Zakarid princes, generals of Tamar's Georgia, had liberated northern Armenia (Ani, Lori, Kars) around 1199-1211. After the Mongol invasion (1236) they governed as vassals of the Ilkhanate, preserving monasteries and scriptoria (Haghpat, Sanahin) under crushing taxation.
