Gilzanu

Year on the map : -800

On the north-western shore of Lake Urmia, the land of Gilzanu is mentioned by the Assyrians from the 9th century BC (Black Obelisk of Shalmaneser III) as a tribute-payer — horses, camels, metals. Peopled by groups close to the North Iranian and Hurrian worlds, it formed a march disputed between Assyria, nascent Urartu and the future Mannaeans, before being absorbed by the latter.
