Proto-Scythian culture

Year on the map : -700

On the steppes north of the Black Sea and the Caucasus, proto-Scythian cultures carry on the Cimmerian world before the Scythians proper cross the Caucasus in turn, pursuing the Cimmerians in the late 7th century BC and contesting control of Near Eastern raids with the Medes and Assyrians (Herodotus IV, Assyrian annals) — a nomadic people without fixed borders; the shown area is approximate.
