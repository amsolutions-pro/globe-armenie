Etruscans and Ligurians

Year on the map : -323

North of Rome, Etruria around 323 BC was a declining confederation of cities — Volterra, Orvieto — its Po valley fringe having already passed to the Celts; neighbouring Liguria remained with the Ligurian tribes. The Greek colonies of Corsica had been gone since the battle of Alalia (c. 540 BC).
