Royaume de Géorgie (apogée de Thamar)

Year on the map : 1200

Under Queen Tamar (1184-1213), Georgia was the leading power of the Caucasus: victories at Shamkor (1195) and Basian (1202), Kars taken (1206/1208), Shirvan a vassal, and the Armenian generals Zakare and Ivane liberated Ani and the northern plateau. The Empire of Trebizond (1204) was founded under her aegis.
