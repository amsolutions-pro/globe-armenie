Royaume hasmonéen

Year on the map : -100

Born of the Maccabean revolt (167 BC) against the Seleucids, independent under Simon (141), the kingdom peaked under Alexander Jannaeus (103-76): Judea, Samaria, Galilee, Idumea and the coast from Jaffa to Dora. Pompey subjected it to Rome in 63 BC (Josephus, 1-2 Maccabees).
