Pays de Nairi

Year on the map : -705

The substrate of the tribal confederations of the high plateau around the kingdom of Menua: the upper-Araxes corridor (Erzurum, Pasinler, Horasan) and the region of Mush, inhabited lands not yet subdued by Urartu. The «Nairi lands» are attested by the Assyrians from the 13th to the 9th century BC as a mosaic of highland principalities, the direct substrate of Urartian unification (Salvini, Zimansky, Diakonoff).
