Persarménie (marzpanat)

Year on the map : 554

After the abolition of the Arsacid monarchy (428), eastern Armenia was governed by Sasanian marzpans from Dvin. Vardan Mamikonian's revolt culminated at Avarayr (451); the treaty of Nvarsak (484) guaranteed freedom of Christianity and the autonomy of the nakharars (Elishe, Ghazar Parpetsi).
