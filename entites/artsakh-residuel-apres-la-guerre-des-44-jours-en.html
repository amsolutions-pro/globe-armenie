Artsakh résiduel (après la guerre des 44 jours)

Year on the map : 2020

The autumn 2020 war (44 days) returned the districts and part of Nagorno-Karabakh to Azerbaijan; a Russian contingent guarded the Lachin corridor. Residual Artsakh, strangled by the blockade of the Lachin corridor (from 12 December 2022), was conquered in September 2023: almost all of its ~100,000 Armenians fled in exodus.
