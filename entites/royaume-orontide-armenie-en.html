Royaume orontide (Arménie)

Year on the map : -300

After Gaugamela (331 BC) the Orontid satraps made themselves kings: the kingdom covered the whole highland (Van basin, Syunik, Artsakh), with Armavir then Yervandashat as capitals. The dynasty reigned until Artaxias I founded the Artaxiad line in 189 BC (Khorenatsi, Strabo XI).
