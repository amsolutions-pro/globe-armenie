Kumme

Year on the map : -705

A Hurrian city-state at the sources of the Tigris, seat of the sanctuary of the storm-god Teshub, mentioned as early as the annals of Shalmaneser III as a tributary buffer disputed between Assyria and Urartu — a status similar to Musasir, but dedicated to a different deity (Kavtaradze, Radner) — approximate area, fluctuating tributary border.
