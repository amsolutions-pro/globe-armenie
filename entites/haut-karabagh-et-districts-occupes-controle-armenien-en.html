Haut-Karabagh et districts occupés (contrôle arménien)

Year on the map : 2010

After the Bishkek ceasefire (May 1994), Armenian forces controlled Nagorno-Karabakh and seven adjacent districts (~13% of Azerbaijan), emptied of their Azeri population; hundreds of thousands displaced on both sides. Neither the self-proclaimed republic nor the status quo was recognized — the Minsk Group negotiated without result until 2020.
