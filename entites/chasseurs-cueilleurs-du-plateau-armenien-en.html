Chasseurs-cueilleurs du plateau arménien

Year on the map : -10000

At the end of the last Ice Age, Epipalaeolithic groups occupied the caves of the highland — the Hrazdan gorges (Lernagog) and the Araxes valley. Obsidian from the volcanoes (Arteni, Gutansar) was already used for tools and traded hundreds of kilometres away.
