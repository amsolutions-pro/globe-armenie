Arménie sous l'Ilkhanat

Year on the map : 1300

The Armenian highland was absorbed into the Ilkhanate of Persia: Mongol censuses and taxation, famines and confiscations, decline of the Zakarid princes. Yet the monasteries remained active — the age of the Gladzor school of miniature — before the anarchy following Abu Said's death (1335).
