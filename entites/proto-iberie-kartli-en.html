Proto-Ibérie (Kartli)

Year on the map : -700

In the early Iron Age (11th-6th centuries BC), central Kartli (near Mtskheta, on the middle Kura) and the Alazani valley (the future Kakheti) were peopled by Kartvelian (proto-Georgian) tribes, heirs to the Colchis-Kartli complex. These were still village communities without a centralized state: the kingdom of Iberia would only form in the 6th-4th centuries BC. The region lay on the periphery of the southern kingdoms (Urartu, Diauehi).
