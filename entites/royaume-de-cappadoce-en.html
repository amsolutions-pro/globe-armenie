Royaume de Cappadoce

Year on the map : -100

Kingdom of the Ariarathids, heir to the Persian satrapy, Hellenized, wavering between Pontus, the Seleucids and Rome. Its capital Mazaca (the future Caesarea) lay at the foot of the Argaeus volcano. Rome made it a province in AD 17 on the death of Archelaus.
